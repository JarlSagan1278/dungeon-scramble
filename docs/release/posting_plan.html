Why (Nov 5): a month of posts brought 58 itch views and 22 plays, and friends and family mostly already played. So every step below reaches people who have never heard of us, and most of them come with a reason to play: a swap (you play theirs), a site where people go to play (Newgrounds), a creator's audience, or a short video. Google needs 12 testers opted in for 14 days before a new personal account can publish; developers trade that on tester-swap communities, so it doesn't depend on people we know either.
Goals by Nov 26: 20 first-time players (strangers) with shared runs · 12+ Android testers in the Play closed test · 8 creator pitches sent, 1–2 videos · 3 short videos posted. Weekly check (Fridays): itch views · plays · runs in the play-data sheet · testers opted in · creator replies.
