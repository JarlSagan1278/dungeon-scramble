**Title:** The dice really roll now

**Picture:** devlog_dice_roll_2.4MB.gif (three lid flicks: up, right, left; 320 px, under itch's 3 MB limit; cut from clip_roll.mp4, tools/media/clip_roll.js). Upload the demo zip 1.42.107 first so the post matches the game.

**Body:**

Flick the chest lid open and the dice now tumble out as little 3D cubes. Each one rolls through its own real faces, the way you flicked (some go sideways), in a wave from the side you flicked from, then lands a hair past its spot, rocks, and settles.

The landing clicks follow the dice too: each die clicks the moment it settles, in what it's made of. Wood knocks, alloy ticks, gilded dice ring and magic dice chime, so you can hear a good tray land before you've read it. A die hidden under a Minion always lands as wood, so the sound never gives it away.

If you play with Reduced Motion on, you keep the old quick flip.
