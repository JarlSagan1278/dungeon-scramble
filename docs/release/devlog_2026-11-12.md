# itch devlog, Nov 12, 2026 (demo 1.42.102) — DRAFT for Jeremy

**Title:** Big turns now hit like big turns

**Picture:** clip_crescendo.gif (13 s: a 17-damage turn on Floor 1; made with tools/media/clip_crescendo.js from the release build). Upload the demo zip 1.42.102 first so the post matches the game.

**Body:**

When you string together a long path, the game now makes a moment of it. After the last die lands, your turn's total slams onto the monster ("17 DAMAGE"), the screen shakes, a chord rings out, and the monster waits a beat before answering. Small turns stay quick. Hit Skip and it's gone.

What else changed since the Floor 2 update:

- **The monster's next hit is exact.** The number under its name is now what you'll actually take before Block, relics included (Gambeson and Warding Bell used to be left out).
- **Losing points you somewhere.** The defeat screen names your next goal: what your first win unlocks, or which relic a win with this origin earns, plus "Furthest yet" when you beat your best.
- **Closing the game mid-choice is safe.** The loot chest, the equip screen and relic picks pick up where you left them, and a turn locks in when you press Go.
- **"Tap me" looks the same everywhere.** Anything waiting on you (the loot chest, doors, the shrine button, a Skill-ready die) sits still and gives a little wiggle every few seconds.
- **"Turn 3 times" is gone.** It was finished by accident in almost every fight, so that poster now asks for Straightedge: four dice in a row along one full row or column.
- Smaller things: Back Pocket saves get their own "Saved!" card, shrine heals say exactly what they'll restore, and camp no longer jumps around when you choose Rest or Train.

The game now asks about sharing play data early in your first run, right after your first loot pick, instead of at the very end. If you can, say yes: it's the only way I can see where new players get stuck. Every comment here gets read.

— Jeremy (and Katlynn, who designed the board game with me)
