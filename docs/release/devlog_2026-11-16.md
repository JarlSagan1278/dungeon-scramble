**Title:** Your loot shows now (and the dice really roll)

**Upload first:** dungeon_scramble_demo_itch_1.42.146.zip (index.html at the zip's root), so the post matches the game.

**Picture:** devlog_dice_roll_2.4MB.gif (docs/release/media; under itch's 3 MB limit). It shows the dice roll from

**Body:**

A lot changed this week, mostly in how the game looks and feels.

**The dice really roll.** Flick the chest open and the dice tumble out as little 3D cubes, rolling the way you flicked, then
land with a click that matches what they're made of: wood knocks, alloy ticks, gilded rings, magic chimes. The dice box is
now a strapped wooden chest with a barrel lid (and a lot more stickers on it).

**Your loot shows.** Your best pieces now appear on your adventurer in fights and on the victory screen: something slung
on your back, things on the floor at your feet, something floating beside you, and one very special hat. In camp you put it
all down.

**So does theirs.** Elite foes and bosses carry the loot die they'll drop, so you can see what you're fighting for. Beat
them and it's one of your choices.

**Losing looks like losing.** The defeat screen shows the room you fell in: you on the floor, your gear scattered, and the
monster that beat you wearing your best piece.

**A real map.** The route at the top is now a torn parchment map. Cleared rooms get crossed off, forks split the path to
two doors, and the maps of the floors you've finished pile up underneath as you go deeper.

**On a computer?** The game now lays itself out for a wide screen (room on the left, dice on the right) and plays with the
keyboard or a controller. It works on the Steam Deck in Chrome too.

As always, if the game asks to share play data at the end of a run, saying yes is the biggest help: it shows us where runs
end and which fights feel unfair.

— Jeremy and Katlynn
