**Body:**

Two weeks of updates, mostly about giving you a reason to come back tomorrow, and about making every turn feel snappier.

**Daily Dungeon.** Every day there's one dungeon, the same for everyone: same rooms, same dice, same luck. You get one real
try, and your result turns into a little share card. Want to warm up first? Practice runs are allowed; only your first try
counts.

**Guild Ranks.** Every run now earns renown, won or lost, and renown raises your Guild Rank: Natural, Whole, Integer,
Rational... all the way to Infinite. Each rank gives a gift to every future run: a Reroll token, extra health, your pick of
relics you've found before.

**Feel clever.** Pull off something smart and the game stamps it: Clever route!, Perfect Block!, Called it! when you freeze
a monster's big hit, Exact! when you kill with not one point of damage wasted.

**Smoother turns.** Rooms open by themselves (tap to hurry them), turns play out quicker, long paths speed up as they go,
and there's a Fast turns switch in Settings if you want it quicker still. The tutorial's speech bubbles now point right at
what they're talking about.

**Easier on the eyes.** Attack dice are a deeper red, so they stand out from the cream Maneuver dice even with red-green
colour blindness. On a slow phone the game switches to lighter effects by itself.

**Android testers wanted!** Dungeon Scramble is coming to Google Play, and Google needs 12 testers to stay in the test for
two weeks before it can launch. If you have an Android phone and want the whole game early: join
https://groups.google.com/g/dungeon-scramble-testers, then opt in at
https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dungeonscramble.game and install it from the Play Store. Keep it installed for 14
days and play now and then. That's all it takes, and it helps more than you'd think.

As always, if the game asks to share play data at the end of a run, saying yes is the biggest help: it shows us where runs
end and which fights feel unfair.
