https://youtu.be/JPX5PqwpdOg

We made a trailer! Everything you hear in it is the game's own music and sound effects.

A lot landed in the last two weeks, mostly about giving you a reason to come back tomorrow, and about making every turn feel snappier.

DAILY DUNGEON
Every day there's one dungeon, the same for everyone: same rooms, same loot, same hero. You get one scored try, and your result turns into a little share card. Practice runs are allowed; only your first try counts.

GUILD RANKS
Every run now earns renown, won or lost, and renown raises your Guild Rank: Natural, Whole, Integer, Rational... all the way to Infinite. Each rank gives a gift to every future run: a Reroll token, extra health, your pick of relics you've found before.

FOES WITH RULES
Some monsters now bend the rules. The Null Hypothesis is immune to a different face every turn. The Odd Permutation rewards paths of the right length. Most Wanted pays a bounty for its Wanted poster. New this week: the Zero-Sum Game heals from the Block you gain (so hit hard instead of hiding), and two more wait on Floor 3 of the full game. Look them up in the Codex once you've met them.

FEEL CLEVER
Pull off something smart and the game stamps it: Clever route!, Perfect Block!, Called it! when you freeze a monster's big hit, Exact! when you kill with not one point of damage wasted. There are new achievements for the Daily, the ranks and the rule-bending foes too.

SMOOTHER TURNS
Rooms open by themselves (tap to hurry them), turns play out quicker, long paths speed up as they go, and there's a Fast turns switch in Settings. The tutorial's bubbles now point right at what they're talking about.

EASIER ON THE EYES
Attack dice are a deeper red, so they stand out from the cream Maneuver dice even with red-green colour blindness. On a slower phone the game switches to lighter effects by itself.

ANDROID TESTERS WANTED
Dungeon Scramble is coming to Google Play, and Google needs 12 testers to stay in the test for two weeks before it can launch. If you have an Android phone and want the whole game early, for free:
1. Join https://groups.google.com/g/dungeon-scramble-testers
2. Opt in at https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dungeonscramble.game
3. Install it from the Play Store link on that page, keep it for 14 days, and play now and then.
That's all it takes, and it helps more than you'd think.

As always, if the game asks to share play data at the end of a run, saying yes is the biggest help: it shows us where runs end and which fights feel unfair.

Jeremy and Katlynn
