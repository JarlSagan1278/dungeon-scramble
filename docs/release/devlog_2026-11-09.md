# itch devlog, Nov 9, 2026 (demo 1.42.72)

**Title:** The free demo now includes Floor 2

**Picture:** devlog_rabbats.png (Floor 2, Rabbid Rabbats, Wanted poster with "Leap to Leap"); made from the demo build.

**Body:**

The free demo now goes two floors deep. Beat the Floor 1 boss and you keep going: new monsters, Gilded loot dice, and a Floor 2 boss before the demo ends.

A playtester recorded a full session last week, and most of this update comes straight from it:

- **New Wanted posters ask for a shape, not just a count.** "Leap to Leap", "Turn 3 times", "Far corners", "Edge path 5+". The highest-damage path isn't always the best one anymore. Hold a poster row to see its full rule.
- **Dodge is now Sidestep.** It never blocked anything; it lets your next step go diagonally, and now the name says so.
- **You can tell whose D.O.T. is whose.** Your status chips are blue, the monster's are red, and when damage over time lands a label floats up: "D.O.T. 2 → You".
- **Boost shows its reach.** After you step on a Boost, the dice it would multiply get a small gold mark; the ones it would be wasted on fade.
- **Class Skills work on blanks.** The Armsmaster and Guard can turn an empty face into a Hit or a Block, and the game now tells you so.
- New one-time tips for Sidestep, D.O.T., your Skill, longer paths, and your first loot pick.

If you play, the game asks at the end of a run whether to share play data. Saying yes is the biggest help right now: it shows where runs end and which fights feel unfair. Every comment here gets read.

— Jeremy (and Katlynn, who designed the board game with me)
