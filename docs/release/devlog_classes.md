# itch devlog draft: five new classes (build 1.42.170) — for Jeremy

**Title:** Five new classes to unlock (and a dog named Pointer)

**Picture:** new_classes.png (the five class cards; sent in chat, Nov 25). Put it at the top.

**Upload first:** nothing to do: the demo on itch updates itself on every release. Note: the demo covers Floors 1-2, so
demo players can unlock the Field Medic and the Trickster through play; the other three come with the full game.

```
Five new classes join the guild! The four you start with stay as they are; these five are earned by playing, and each
one brings a signature companion that stands beside your hero all run.

FIELD MEDIC (play 10 runs)
Turn a die in your bottom row into a Heal, and half of any healing past full becomes Block. A gentler way in if the
dungeon has been rough on you.

TRICKSTER (play 5 Daily Dungeons)
Sleight of Hand: swap a corner die with any other die, once a turn. Pull the good dice into reach.

RANGER AND POINTER (reach the Rational Guild Rank)
Pointer, a very good grey schnauzer, lands on a die each turn and gives the dice beside him +1. Call Fetch! and he
runs two more steps after your path and uses those dice too.

ALCHEMIST (beat every foe that follows a rule)
Turn a centre die into poison, and every D.O.T. you play is stronger. Brew early; it ticks every turn.

ENGINEER (win at Depth 3)
Print: copy any die's face onto your left column, and it stays printed for the whole fight. Build your machine one
turn at a time.

ALSO NEW
- 19 new achievements, with bronze, silver and gold milestones (Overkills, Exact! kills, Depth wins, and a few feats
  like 10 Maneuvers in one path).
- Tap the Wanted poster in a fight to read its quests up close.
- A kerning and padding pass on every screen, smoother setup on PC, and a lighter load on your phone's battery when
  the game sits idle.
- They say something waits for anyone who looks too long into a fountain's water...

As always, if the game asks to share play data at the end of a run, saying yes is the biggest help.

Jeremy (and Katlynn, who designed the board game with me)
```
