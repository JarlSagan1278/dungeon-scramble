# First-run difficulty and quick-start bug hunt (Oct 28, 1.41.94)

Why: 1.41.94 makes a device's first run a Dwarf Armsmaster that starts straight in the guided fight. Does a newcomer
survive Floor 1 (the whole demo)?

## Study
tools/balance/harness.js, Dwarf Armsmaster, depth 0, **random loot picks** (a newcomer doesn't know which die is good),
150 runs per bot effort level (skill = path-search effort; 15 ≈ grabs a short obvious path).

| Bot effort | Dies on Floor 1 | Reaches Floor 2 | Full win | Floor-1 deaths before the boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 (weakest) | 38% | 62% | 2% | 25 of 57 |
| 60 | 16% | 84% | 17% | 8 of 24 |
| 300 | 6% | 94% | 29% | 1 of 9 |
| 1500 | 13% | 87% | 24% | 5 of 19 |

- Nobody dies in the first fight at any level (the guided Filement Bunny room is safe).
- On Floor 1 the boss is the wall: the three Floor 1 bosses (Heating Elemental, Rogue Golemancer, Grosteque Grafter)
  account for about 70% of Floor-1 deaths, evenly split. Only the weakest bot dies in regular rooms in any number
  (fights 3-5: Unbound Automa, Amanitabomination, Leper Colonizers).
- In the demo a boss death still means the player saw the whole floor, so the demo experience holds up.
- Random loot costs a lot later on (24-29% full wins vs 56% with best picks in the Oct 16 baseline), which is
  fine for a first run.

Recommendation: no change. Watch real demo data for deaths in fights 2-4; if real newcomers die there more than
~15%, soften those rooms on depth 0 first.
