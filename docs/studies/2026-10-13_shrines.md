# Shrine value (Oct 13, 2026, build 1.41.2)

Method: regular-player bot, Depth 0, 4 origin/class pairs x 200 runs per shrine. To get a clear signal, every eligible room is a shrine (`shrineAll`, about 2 shrines per floor instead of the usual ~0.8), and the tested shrine replaces any shrine drawn on its own floor (`forceShrine`). Each row is the win-rate change against the same setup with the normal shrine mix (56.9%). Because the shrine shows up twice and replaces fights (fights give loot), the effects are amplified: read the order and the big gaps, not the exact sizes. Noise about +/-3.

Findings:
- **Relic shrines dominate on Floors 1-2** (+10 to +31): a relic early pays off all run. On Floor 3 they're worth little (+2 to +7) because the run is almost over; healing / max HP is what helps there.
- **Token shrines (Scroll Dispensers) are the weakest shrines**, even when the bot uses every token it gets (-22 to -27 on Floors 1-2). A one-use token is worth far less than a relic or max HP.
- **Die-upgrade shrines are weak** (Expediting Spores 0; Enchanted Anvil -13): one die one level up is small next to a relic.
- **Loot relics are weak everywhere** (Loot-Diagonalizer +1, Madpie Nest -8): loot help matters less than the bot's other options; on Floor 3 it comes too late.
- **Deal shrines are fair when taken:** Blood-Drive Treats +6, Time-Intensive 0, Faustian -3. The bot's own rule (never drop below 20 max HP) makes it walk away from Blood-Drive on Floor 1, so their regular-run scores understate them.

Possible tweaks (not applied): Scroll Dispensers give more charges or add a small heal; Enchanted Anvil and Expediting Spores upgrade two dice (or upgrade + heal); Madpie Nest (Floor 3) becomes an offense/defense relic shrine or a healing one; Floor 3 relic shrines could offer 4 choices. Union Dues interplay not measured yet.
