# Loot dice value (Oct 13, 2026, build 1.41.0)

Method: each loot die replaces one Attack Die I at the start of every run (like the relic study's forced relics), regular-player bot, Depth 0, 4 origin/class pairs x 120 runs per die. Win rate vs the same runs without it (base 70.8%). Noise is about +/-3 points per die, so only gaps of 5+ points mean much. Forcing a Gilded or Magic die from Floor 1 overstates its value, so compare dice within a tier.

Patterns:
- **Sustain and Block lead every tier** (Medsack's four Heal 2s, Triage Turret, Exile Elixir's Block 4s, the armors and shields). Same finding as the relic study: healing and Block decide runs.
- **Fumble dice are the weakest damage dice** (Acid Potion, Claymore): the Fumble face hurts more than their extra damage helps.
- **Movement and Swap-heavy dice sit at the bottom** (Winged Boots, Genetic Drifter, Grappling Set, Tarot Deck, Domesticated Displacer). Part of this is the bot (it gets little out of Swap), but it matches the old Maneuver finding.
- **Medsack is an outlier** in Alloy (+20 vs a +8 median): four Heal 2 faces on a Floor 1 die.

Candidate tweaks (not applied): Medsack Heal2 x4 -> Heal2 x3 + blank; Acid Potion and Claymore drop the Fumble for a blank (or keep the Fumble and raise a hit by 1); Winged Boots / Genetic Drifter / Domesticated Displacer turn their blank into a small Block or Heal. The Suggested die (1.40.1) uses face weights that already rank these dice in roughly this order.

## Identity-keeping tweaks, tested (same method, 480 runs per die)
Jeremy asked whether the first tweaks made dice too similar to others (yes for Claymore = Greathammer, Acid Potion ~ Greataxe, Winged Boots ~ Tarot Deck), so each fix leans into the die's own theme instead.

| Die | New faces | Before | After | Tier median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claymore | Hit 2 x3, Combo 2, Fumble, blank | +4 | +6 | +8 |
| Acid Potion | Hit 1, Hit 2, D.O.T. 2 x2, Fumble, blank | +1 | +8 | +8 |
| Medsack | Heal 1, Heal 2 x3, blank x2 | +20 | +16 | +8 (still the top Alloy die) |
| Winged Boots | Combo 3, Dodge, Leap, Pivot, Nullify, Boost x2 | +7 | +12 | +13 |
| Genetic Drifter | Combo 1, Leech 1 x2, Pivot, Swap, Nullify | +7 | +14 | +13 |
| Domesticated Displacer | Swap x2, Combo 3, Combo 2 x2, Pivot | +11 | +17 | +18 |

All six land at or near their tier's median (noise about +/-3), and none copies another die.
