## Big hits and one-shots (1.41.0)
Per fight, the bot now logs the most HP lost to hits in a single enemy turn, and the HP it had when a fatal turn began. A "one-shot" here = dying in one enemy turn that started at 50%+ of max HP.

| Data | One-shot share of deaths | Worst single enemy turn, median / 95th pct / max (F1, F2, F3) |
|---|---|---|
| Regular, Depth 0 | 3% (23 of 874) | 2/7/9, 3/9/13, 6/14/45 |
| New, Depth 0 | 2% (36 of 1558) | 2/7/9, 3/9/17, 6/16/53 |
| Expert, depth study (all loadouts) | 5% (381 of 7599) | 2/7/12, 3/9/18, 5/13/69 |

- One-shots are rare; Floors 1-2 never produce a turn bigger than 18.
- The tails are on Floor 3's bosses and Anti-Euclidian Space: a 25+ damage turn happens in 0.8-1.6% of those fights (max 45-69). Most are minion-scaled specials (Myceleus "Be Fruitful and Multiply!", the Wizard's "Lily Pod": 3 damage per Minion, and Swarming at depth fills the tray) or a long Extruzeon fight.
- Extruzeon's cost tracks fight length as designed: 1-4 turns 8.8 HP, 5-6 turns 13.9, 7-8 turns 20.3, 9-11 turns 27.6, 12+ turns 45.9.
- Optional guard if those tails feel unfair in play: cap damage-per-Minion specials at, e.g., 8 Minions counted (24 damage), and/or cap Extruzeon's layers at +12.

- Fights are short overall (2-4 turns; bosses 5-7); 5% of fights end in one turn.
- **Floor 2's Easy rooms are the slowest non-boss fights in the game** (4.5 turns for regular players vs 3.4 for Floor 2 Standard): they're easy because they hit softly, but they have a lot of HP. Regular player, turns and HP lost: The Warcart 5.1 / 4.2 (38 HP after floor scaling), Armored Gear-Pest 4.8 / 3.6 (35), Roudy Rinotter 4.5 / 3.9 (30), Adhesive Pest Traps 4.4 / 3.8 (45), Unventilated Workshop 4.4 / 3.7 (45), Ditched Heist (Standard) 4.2 / 3.0 (45). They cost little HP, so they read as a grind rather than a threat. Fix: about 25-30% less HP on those cards, same damage (aim: ~3 turns like Floor 2 Standard).
- **Myceleus is the longest fight**: 7.7 turns for regular players, and 52% of its fights take 8+ turns (185 HP after floor scaling, plus Shield). The other Big Bosses take 6.5. Option: about 12% less HP (106 -> 93 base) to bring it to ~6.8 turns; it is also the deadliest Big Boss (9.3% for regular players), so this would soften it a little.
