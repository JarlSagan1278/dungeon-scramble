# Relics and shrines

Relic tiers, gating, gem look and names; the shrine overhaul, shrine value rule and shrine flow. Jeremy's decisions, oldest first, with dates and the build they shipped in. Split out of PROJECT_CONTEXT.md on Oct 28, 2026; add new decisions at the bottom.

- Shrines have no tier: a Shrine simply takes the place of an easy or standard fight (never an elite or deadly one), at most one per segment.
- They lack presence in animation, timing and execution. Keep rarity but greatly increase quantity as a way to add more relics. Relic idea: conversion (e.g. 1 damage per Shield gained).
- **Shrine overhaul decisions (Oct 11, 2026):** full shrine ritual (shrine entrance, press-and-hold activation, rewards land where they live); about 10 shrine cards per floor with roughly half giving relics (themed relic shrines); bargain shrines (a relic for a price) yes; keep all 12 new relics for now but balance them later, especially against the origin-unlocked relics; shrine frequency stays at today's base and rises as a run goes on (by floor) and with depth.
- Shipped in 1.36.0: 30 shrines (16 new, using stand-in art borrowed from related shrines until his art exists), 23 general relics, shrine chance 40% / 45% / 50% per eligible room on Floors 1/2/3, +3% per depth level above 1 (max 70%).
- **Relic look (Jeremy, Oct 12):** relics as literal items overlap with loot-dice concepts, and drawing 27 items is a lot of art; the two-letter badge feels too cheap long term. Find a more abstract representation. Relic art is on hold until then.
- **Relic look mockups (Oct 12):** wax seals, tarot cards, constellations (all code), gem set with a socket bracelet; source in git history (docs/mockups/relic_looks.html, removed Oct 28). Waiting on Jeremy's pick.
- **Relic look: gems (Jeremy, Oct 12):** relics become cut gems: cut + color = family (trillion offense, emerald-cut defense, hexagon path, round loot/tempo, kite origin), glyph etched on top; optional socket bracelet. In-game mockup: docs/mockups/relic_gems_ingame.js (git history). Jeremy (Oct 12): full-size gems in the buffs row are too big (they push the hero up and crowd buffs); compared 18px gems in the row vs a gem column beside the hero (docs/mockups/relic_gems_hud.js, git history), waiting on his pick. Next: Jeremy makes item-y relic names more conceptual or gem-y (flags + ideas in the Relics tab, column I).
- **Gem relics shipped in 1.39.0 (Jeremy, Oct 12):** side-column placement beside the hero in fights (his pick). Family = `relicFamily(r)` (origin, else first tag; tempo counts as loot). Glyphs live in `RELIC_GLYPH` (render.js), gems in `relicGemSVG`. Note: defense is the biggest family (10 blue gems). Relic art files are no longer needed.
- **11 relic renames from Claude's flags (Jeremy approved all, Oct 12):** see CHANGELOG. Filement Bunny ENCOUNTER_SIZE .53 -> .75 (Jeremy: bigger to see details).
- **Relic power and gating (Jeremy, Oct 12):** varying relic power is fine, but a large relic study is needed, and some batches of relics may be quietly gated behind achievements to space out the power gain over many runs (not a visible unlock tree).
- **Relic study decisions (Jeremy, Oct 12, after 1.39.1):** (1) the S/A/B/C tiers are approved; the strongest relics only need testing at deep depths, which needs a study of how difficulty scales with depth. (2) Gating, not nerfs, for shared relics; EXCEPT origin relics, which must all be weak or solid at best (tier B or below: Stronger Infill +43, Faerie Fire +27 and Coffee Break +21 come down; Mycorrhizal Bond +11 is fine). (3) The duds get no extra handling beyond this for now. Also: the bot must use tokens and pick loot well so every relic and loot die can be measured.
- **Relic families evened (Jeremy, Oct 12):** first tags switched for 6 relics so gem families are 6/6/5/6; a 6th path relic later would make it even.
- **Relic study at depth 7 and 11 (Oct 12):** tiers re-set from data (S: Scavenger's Acumen, Evasion Tank, Minus-One Mantra; A: Hard Hat Act, Perfect Fifth, Prime Edge). Origin relics brought to tier B per Jeremy's rule: Stronger Infill 2 Block (+21; 1 Block tested +9), Coffee Break H.O.T. +1 only (+13), Faerie Fire capped at +2 per path (+12). Bottom five relics (≤ +3) are rework candidates if Jeremy wants. Report: the 2026-10-12 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md).
- **Shrine rule (Jeremy, Oct 14):** every shrine must be at least as good as a same-floor loot pick (the knockout study found a shrine room worth less than a fight room, because fights give loot; giving every shrine a full loot pick on top overshot by +26). Next: measure each shrine against a "loot-pick shrine" and raise the ones below the bar.
- **Relics (Jeremy, Oct 12):** keep the five low-value relics (FTLeporter, Inductive Step, Equal and Opposite, Power Word: Safe, Bloodthirst) -- they're fun and change strategy. Equal and Opposite: test reflecting on any Blocked hit, not only fully Blocked ones. New depth levers to test: Union Dues (shrine boon costs 2 HP) and Rounding Error (Heal faces heal 1 less, min 1). Swap/Reroll after-use: testing "step" vs "pivot" (Jeremy's idea: used die works like a Pivot). New-player experience = the base dungeon with no modifiers (Jeremy calls it depth 0; the game labels it Depth 1).
- **Decisions (Jeremy, Oct 13):** Equal and Opposite reflects the Blocked amount on every Blocked hit (+6 at depth 7; 'any hit, 2 back' was +5, old rule ~0). Union Dues and Rounding Error stay as one-point depth levers (−2.3 and −3.9 win points alone).
- **Shrines:** every shrine at least as good as a same-floor loot pick. Boost each weak shrine's own reward first; give a loot pick on top only where it fits the theme, and sparingly. Applied in 1.41.23 after three test rounds (the 2026-10-15 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md)): loot picks went to the Scroll Dispensers (they dispense a die; extra scrolls alone barely helped), Madpie Nest (a hoard) and Storied Emporium (a shop); the Enchanted Anvil can forge a new die.
- **Shrine overhaul is done** (Jeremy, Oct 21): the shrine ritual, 30 shrines, 12 new relics and shrine boosts.
- **Shrine flow (Jeremy, Oct 16, 1.41.34):** the Hold button is a round button over the dice tray that invites a press (pulse, filling ring); Continue uses the same circle; shrine loot opens directly under the shrine's name (no chest, no "Victory!"); relic-plus-loot shrines move on to the loot automatically.
- **Shrine buttons and floor badge (Jeremy, Oct 16, 1.41.37):** the shrine's round Hold button sits in the dead centre of the screen (not the dice box); Continue is green, Hold stays gold; shrines wiggle when tapped like monsters. The round bubble always names the floor: "F2 · Rd 3" (Rd, not R, so it can't read as Room), "F2 · Shrine" at shrines.
- **Relic gems take part (Jeremy, Oct 16, 1.41.38):** when a relic acts and its gem is on screen, the gem itself reacts (a copy swells/wobbles/brightens over it and settles back), so it's clear which relic joined in. Every relic that changes play now reports when it acts, including the path-rule ones (Combonatorics, FTLeporter).
- **Relic gem outline (Jeremy, Oct 16, 1.41.39):** gems get a thicker black outline (9 of 112 units) even though they're small -- the chunky-outline look holds at every size.
