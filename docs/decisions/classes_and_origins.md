# Classes and origins

Class zones, Skills and passives; origin balance and origin dice. Jeremy's decisions, oldest first, with dates and the build they shipped in. Split out of PROJECT_CONTEXT.md on Oct 28, 2026; add new decisions at the bottom.

- Origin strength (bot, 250 runs per origin × class, 1.37.1): Halfling 67% wins, Dwarf 58%, Gnome 49%, Pixie 46%. Classes: Dungeoneer 61%, Arcanist 58%, Armsmaster 51%, Guard 50%. Healing (Halfling's Bonus Breakfast) is the strongest starting tool.
- **Origin balance (Oct 11):** some imbalance is fine, especially for origins you unlock first, but **Pixie should be clearly stronger**. Pixie Powder should get a Swap face (planned: Swap replaces Dodge, once Swap exists).
- Origin win rates after 1.38.0 (bot, 250 runs per origin x class): Halfling 71%, Dwarf 60%, Gnome 48%, Pixie 47% (was 46%). The bot gets little out of Swap, so Pixie needs another lever.
- **Pixie buff (Oct 11, 1.38.1):** Jeremy picked option 1: Pixie Powder Combo 1 → Combo 2 and D.O.T. 1 → D.O.T. 2. Bot win rate 47% → 54% (Halfling 71%, Dwarf 60%, Gnome 48%).
- **Class x origin study (Oct 12; Jeremy asked whether origin or class carries the wins): class does.** At depth 7, class explains 96% of the win-rate spread (Guard 89%, Armsmaster 67%, Dungeoneer 42%, Arcanist 40%); origin, including the origin die, only 3% (10-point range). Origin relics were not part of it. Report: the 2026-10-12 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md).
- **Class changes (Jeremy, Oct 12):** Guard converts to **Block 1** (was Block 2); Dungeoneer's zone is now the whole left and right edges, so the path **wraps on every row** (was the two middle rows). **Arcanist (Jeremy, Oct 12):** zone is both diagonals and any step into or out of the zone may go diagonally (29% -> 53% at depth 7, next to Armsmaster). **Guard / Block feel (Jeremy, Oct 12):** Block's power is early-game (Floor 1 deaths 33% -> 0%) and invisible, so show prevented damage more clearly using existing UI: a blue "N blocked" tick floater on the HP bar + a Damage blocked run-summary chip. Class ability uses are now in telemetry (`class_ability`) to compare Jeremy's usage with the bot's.
- **Study follow-ups (Jeremy, Oct 12):** origin mattering little is fine ("players should play who they want aesthetically"); sustain winning is fine because every class/origin can get sustain (loot Heal/H.O.T./Leech/Block dice, shrines, camp, relics); **Stronger Infill -> 1 Block** (+9); Jeremy uses the class ability every turn, so the bot's class numbers apply to his play. Zeal now shows in the enemy's next-turn warning (preview = real hit).
- **Dungeoneer left as is (Jeremy, Oct 14),** even after the wrap fix: experts at internal depth 7 win 22% with it (Arcanist 34%, Armsmaster 45%, Guard 50%), but it stays one of the easiest classes for new and regular players. The two tested boosts (all four edges wrap, or a once-a-turn zone die -> Pivot Skill) both reach about 35-36% if revisited. the 2026-10-14 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md).
- **Dungeoneer wrap rule (1.41.8, from tester feedback):** across the left/right seam each face keeps its own move rule (step = same row, Dodge = diagonal across the seam, Pivot = either, Leap can't use it). Before this, any die in the edge zone could also step straight up/down, so the bot studies slightly overrated the Dungeoneer. Confirm with Jeremy that the diagonal-across-the-seam Dodge is what he intends.
- **Armsmaster colour: violet** (#7a45d6), decided Oct 15; its Skill button had the same red as Clear.
- **Arcanist rule changed (Jeremy, Oct 16, 1.41.47):** diagonal steps only from an X square to an X square (`diagonalZone:'both'`); onto/off the X uses normal moves. Replaces the Oct 12 'into or out of' rule. Jeremy chose this knowing the Oct 12 study had it at 29% vs 53% at depth 7 (no compensating buff for now).
- **Arcanist +1 Boost (study Oct 16; applied by Jeremy in 1.41.53, `boostPlus:1`, `classFace()` in logic.js):** experts at depth 7, 1,200 runs per class: Arcanist today 34%, +1 to all Boosts 49%, +1 only on X dice 45%, old into/out-of rule 48%; Guard 66%, Armsmaster 57%, Dungeoneer 36%. Regular players at depth 0: today 68%, +1 79% (easiest class). the 2026-10-16 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md).
- **Dungeoneer wrap bonus (study Oct 16; option B applied by Jeremy in 1.41.54, `seamDamage:1`, `applyClassToSteps()` in logic.js):** experts at depth 7: today 36%, A +1 Block faces 51%, B wrap crossing gives +1 to next damaging die 55%, C +1 Hit/Combo on edges 69%, D Maneuvers give +1 Block 94% (edge-only 74%); Arcanist 50%, Armsmaster 54%, Guard 62%. Regulars at depth 0: today 76%, A/B 85%. Recommended B. the 2026-10-16 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md).
- **Class baseline (Oct 17, build 1.41.54, 1,200 runs per class per cell):** experts depth 7: Guard 63%, Armsmaster 57%, Dungeoneer 55%, Arcanist 50% (36% of Arcanist deaths on Floor 1); regulars depth 0: Dungeoneer 83%, Arcanist 79%, Guard 70%, Armsmaster 68%. Built before the Boost-token fix (1.41.56). the 2026-10-17 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md).
- **Guard zone (Jeremy, Oct 25, 1.41.86):** back to a full 2x2 square, middle right (was the 3-square L since Oct 12; the L took the Guard from 63% to 58% for experts at depth 7, so expect it a little stronger again). Block stays 1.
