# Rules and dice

Core rules: minions, Shield and Block, loot and boons, Swap/Reroll, the Back Pocket, campfire, quests, Freeze, Maneuver and basic dice. Jeremy's decisions, oldest first, with dates and the build they shipped in. Split out of PROJECT_CONTEXT.md on Oct 28, 2026; add new decisions at the bottom.

- Enemy Shield absorbs damage the player deals. Block and all statuses reset when a new room opens (no carryover unless a relic grants it).
- Minion dice: cleared one per point of damage dealt, plus any minion jumped over by Leap; never carried between fights. Minions are placed right after the roll (before the player can tell what was rolled there). A minion covers a face that is revealed only while a path resolves, so Maneuvers cannot scout; landing on a covered space ends the path (it is blank). Leap/Dodge/Pivot may land on a covered cell. Using a class ability on a minion cell removes the minion. Minions look like dice with small creepy eyes at random spots and skews, greyed like unreachable dice during pathing.
- Loot dice join the Compendium the first time you get one; 3 copies (reduced from 5, "for now") unlock it as a Starting Boon, because boons were too strong and the game is already potentially too easy. End-of-run screens should announce newly unlocked boons.
- Pivot → Boost on high-level Maneuver dice was studied (same proposal doc). **Decision (Oct 11): keep Pivot**; revisit with the Maneuver meta.
- Maneuver dice: he worries Maneuver dice/actions have no point compared to Attack. Studied Oct 11, 2026 (the 2026-10-11 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md)): they are a net loss today and the v1.32.76 tester dropped them first. **Decision (Oct 11): keep the analysis but wait on more play data before drastically changing the Maneuver meta.** Leading candidate when revisited: Dodge Block (each Dodge gives 1 Block); Leap Momentum (+1 to the next damaging die) is the smallest parity fix. Adding Boost to an upgraded Maneuver die is a last resort only. Overlapping, stackable effects that cover slightly different stats are good design.
- Wants a way to compare dice in the loot "equip and choose which to replace" picker (later). Wants flavor text for loot dice (later; not in the test build). Add back reroll faces and swap faces.
- **Swap and Reroll rules (Jeremy):** Reroll lets you reroll ONE die of your choice. Swap lets you swap the Swap face's die with one die of your choice (they trade places on the tray). Claude's assumption, to confirm: each is used by tapping it before building your path, and the die is then spent for the turn.
- **Swap/Reroll timing confirmed:** tap the face before building your path; that die is then spent for the turn. Shrines can also give Swap and Reroll tokens that work the same way (like Freeze tokens). Claude's list of edge cases and recommended defaults is in the 2026-10-11 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md), waiting on Jeremy.
- **Swap/Reroll defaults approved (Oct 11):** the used die acts as a blank for the rest of the turn, and a Swap token swaps any two dice. Jeremy likes that these give vectors to buff them later.

- **Back Pocket revive (Jeremy, Oct 14, 1.41.16):** a save spends the WHOLE Back Pocket (roll Pocket dice in random order, first non-blank saves at 5 HP, then the Pocket is empty). Chosen over "save at half max HP" (76/59/49%) and "spend half the Pocket" on purpose: "a good way to bring down the win rate a bit in line with other roguelikes" (bots: regular 56%, new 38%, expert depth 7 27%). Switch: REVIVE_RULE in logic.js.
