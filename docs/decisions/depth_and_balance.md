# Depth and balance

The depth system and its modifiers, dice finishes, and the balance philosophy behind tuning. Jeremy's decisions, oldest first, with dates and the build they shipped in. Split out of PROJECT_CONTEXT.md on Oct 28, 2026; add new decisions at the bottom.

- Difficulty system is called **depth** (earlier "heat"; Hades-style). Class badge shows the highest depth cleared; dice finish upgrades unlock for all classes after a certain depth. He wants a "carrot": a score with a place for player names, plus scalable cosmetics. Balance tuning (reducing one-shot kills on higher floors) comes before depth.
- **Balance bot made player-like (Jeremy approved gaps 1-4, Oct 12):** tokens, face-value loot scoring, class ability every turn, deliberate shrine/relic picks, Overkill valued, all quest rewards. Result: at depth 1 the bot wins 97-100% (Dwarf Armsmaster/Guard; was 68/78%). The class ability is the single biggest lever. So depth 1 is nearly solved for a skilled player; relics must be measured at a depth where the bot wins ~50% (depth study first, then relic study there; tier S at deeper depths). All win-rate numbers before this date used a weaker bot.
- **Depth study recommendations (Jeremy, Oct 12): "do all recommendations."** Depth modifiers reshaped so each depth point costs about the same (~6 win points for a skilled player): Swarming ranks cost 1/3/4; Zeal rank 1 = +1 damage on heavy turns and boss specials only, rank 2 = +1 on every hit; Dread also gives Elite/Deadly foes +20% HP per rank; Burden of Choice removes one loot option per rank (never below one); Rationing Rations a third less per rank (rank 3: rest heals nothing); Clumsy 4 max HP per rank. Being verified by rerunning the depth study.
- **Depth recalibrated (Oct 12):** final ranks and costs in depth.js; every rank costs about 4-7.5 win points per depth point for the skilled bot; depth 9+ is now hard for it. Report: the 2026-10-12 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md) (Recalibration).
- **Night Shift (Jeremy, Oct 14, 1.41.14):** "keep consistent access to shrines". Rank 1 = Easy -> Standard only; rank 2 = Easy and Standard -> Elite; shrine chances use the room's normal tier, so Night Shift never removes shrines. The rulebook is the PDF only (generated by tools/gen.js); the Word copy was dropped.
- **Dice finishes (Jeremy, Oct 14):** unlock at shown depth **3 / 5 / 7 / 9** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Royal; internal FINISH_AT 4/6/8/10), replacing 0/3/6/10. The finish art must not cover other die elements: rim-only, small corners. Jeremy picked the "middle" weight (1.41.11): thicker rim, bigger diamonds/plates/gems, corner scrolls small enough to clear the longest names.
- **More study decisions (Jeremy, Oct 12):** Guard: test a 3-square zone (2 may be too small for strategy); Dungeoneer unchanged for now; Swarming 3 costs more (rank 3 now +6 depth, total 10); wants a cheaper new depth lever (ideas offered: Pop Quiz, Union Dues, Rounding Error, Overtime); dice-finish tiers stay at 4/7/11 as a final challenge -- depth 1 is the new-player experience, and between-run starting dice loadouts will help players climb.
- **Swarming rank 2 costs 2 depth (Jeremy, Oct 13, 1.41.2).** Shrine value study requested and running.
- Dungeon Scramble is mostly single-player, so it does not need competitive-level balance. Some runs should peter out on bad loot; others should hit crazy combos, and those are what players remember. Learning, failing and pure luck are part of a roguelike; the experience should not be too smooth.
- **Spikiness is fun, but the domain matters:** an over-strong player piece (loot die, relic, combo) is fine and often good; an over-strong enemy is much less fun. Cap enemy spikes and unfair deaths; leave player power spikes alone unless they break the game.
- Use studies to find broken outliers and dead content (things nobody would pick), not to flatten everything to the median. Next: a literature review of the genre and industry on where balance is and is not needed.
- **Balance literature review (Oct 13):** the 2026-10-13 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md) (notes in git history). Built from search excerpts only (full pages were blocked); re-check quotes before citing. Supports Jeremy's philosophy; ends with 14 guidelines and a measurement list (human floor funnel, first-10-runs win rate, take rate when offered, avoidable vs unavoidable deaths).
- **Depth modifiers:** reprice only the ones that are really off. Working out the cheapest set of modifiers for yourself is part of the fun, so modest unevenness is fine. Applied 1.41.24: Rounding Error costs 2 depth, Rationing rank 2 costs 1 (the 2026-10-15 study (summary in docs/STUDY_FINDINGS.md)).
- **Reward for a new best depth:** already covered by the dice finishes and relic gating; nothing new.
- **Assist mode that grows after losses:** not for now.
- **Zeal rank 1 (Jeremy, Oct 22): the code is right** — rank 1 adds +1 to every enemy hit (rank 2 adds +1 more on heavy turns and boss specials). The Oct 12 note saying "heavy turns and specials only" for rank 1 is superseded.
