# Look and feel

Visual direction, textures, layout rules, monster footing, onboarding (splash, guided fight, quick start, tips), the chest lid, performance and accessibility. Jeremy's decisions, oldest first, with dates and the build they shipped in. Split out of PROJECT_CONTEXT.md on Oct 28, 2026; add new decisions at the bottom.

- Target phone: 395x769 game card in a 411x785 viewport; the card must never grow taller.
- "Toy-like": chunky outlines, dark-brown base with light-brown windows. Never the same color next to itself; each button its own color. One consistent outline color; larger dice with little dead space.
- Metamorphous for titles, plain serif for everything else (he dislikes mixed fonts).
- Tray buttons sit in a side rail beside the dice tray (not a row); the portrait slightly overlaps the tray. Dice tray is the "Oak & Iron" chest. Combo-power outline is shaped like the star icon.
- Rejected: darker/ornate look; room dressing such as banners and skulls (for now); a "first Block die +1" bonus.
- Fight scene floor line (Oct 15): the wall/floor seam sits 118px down the face-off card on his phone (`ROOM_MIN_HORIZON`, was 134), as high as it can go while the tallest 3-quest sign stays on the wall. Monster heights are then set one by one on the Monster Footing page (https://claude.ai/artifact/6sxozXQfah2dSsRxnQEAkj), because some monsters float.
- Monster facing and size (Oct 15): Toadtaur Warrior and Rabbid Rabbats are flipped; Eelagator Escapee shrunk to a normal size. Monsters always stand in front of the Quests sign. Per-monster heights set on the Monster Footing page live in `ENCOUNTER_DROP` (data.js, % of picture height).
- Dice upgrades look like filigreed borders and emblazoned corners, not glows or auras. He likes the wax-seal style victory badge and wants the best badge per class shown on the title screen (earned badges only; no placeholders or names). Badge redesign so each class is represented visually is a to-do.

- **Scramble flick and depth presets (Jeremy, Oct 24, 1.41.83):** each turn's roll is tactile: the dice sit covered until the player flicks the Scramble button (replaces Go until the roll) or the tray, or shakes the phone. A Settings toggle turns it off (Settings rows were tightened so it still doesn't scroll). Depth presets: −/+ on the Depth bar pick `depthPreset(n)` (DEPTH_PRESET_ORDER in depth.js, gentlest ranks first; preset N always totals depth N); individual modifiers still adjustable. Open for Jeremy: tap-to-roll as well as flick? flick threshold and shake strength after phone testing; preset order.
