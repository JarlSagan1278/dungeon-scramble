# Playtest: We Playtest Games, tester "wndg" (Nov 8, 2026)

Free 30-minute recorded session (demo 1.42.66-ish, Floor 1), found via a comment on our r/playmygame post.
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Di-MKuHqH0c · Report: https://app.weplaytestgames.com/transcript/40e623c3-b6b6-46c0-aa02-9eec3814f85f (behind their login)

## Their key takeaways (verbatim)
1. The tutorial doesn't teach the core mechanics well enough: the player was still confused several fights in about how dodge defends, what the class skill's dash-zone replacement does, and whether longer paths are worth anything. They said it took several rounds to understand the core loop and that nothing beyond the basics was explained.
2. Damage-over-time effects are unclear: the player could never tell whether Fumble or Acid Potion was on them or on the enemy, couldn't find the DoT they had applied, didn't know what the '11' number meant, and still didn't understand DoT by the boss fight. Clear status icons, targets, and numbers are needed on both combatants.
3. Turns feel low on strategy: there are usually only one or two workable routes, and the best choice is just the highest-damage path. Maneuver dice like leap, pivot and dodge often felt useless or forced, and players frequently got stuck unable to reach the dice they wanted (for example, Boost with only a dodge next to it, or the Nullifier being out of reach).

## Tester notes (summary)
Liked: the visual style, the unusual dice-path mechanic, simple controls, the satisfaction of a big path. Clicked after a few encounters.
Wanted: clearer explanations of dice effects and how they interact; more situations where different strategies are viable instead of one obvious best route; Floor 1 felt quick once understood (hopes for more floors/challenges).

## Our read (Claude, Nov 8)
- "How dodge defends": the name Dodge reads as a defensive move, but it only moves the path diagonally.
- Acid Potion has DOT2 faces (on the enemy) AND a Fumble1 face (on you) on the same die, with the same D.O.T. icon: the target is genuinely ambiguous.
- The "11" is not identified yet (needs the video timestamp).
